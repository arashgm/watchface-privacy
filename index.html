Watch face privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short: our watch faces do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything they show is processed on your watch and never leaves it.

This policy applies to every Wear OS watch face published by Ruk Gamestudio on Google Play ("the watch faces") and explains how they handle your information.

Developer and legal entity

These watch faces are published on Google Play by Ruk Gamestudio (the "developer", "we", "us"), the developer account named in each Google Play store listing. This policy applies to every watch face published under that developer account, and the developer name above matches the one shown on those listings.

Contact: developer1860@gmail.com

Information we collect

None. The watch faces do not collect personal information, do not require an account, and do not send any data to us or to third parties. They contain no analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Data shown on the watch face

Depending on the design, a watch face may display information such as time, date, battery level, steps, or heart rate. This information is read from your watch's sensors or from complications you choose, and it is used only to draw the watch face. It stays on your device and is not stored or transmitted.

Permissions

Some watch faces may request the following permissions, used only for the purpose described:

  • Physical activity: to show your step count on the watch face.
  • Body sensors: to show your heart rate on the watch face.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your watch settings. The watch face keeps working without the related information.

Third-party complications

If you add complications from other apps, those apps provide the data and are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The watch faces do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
